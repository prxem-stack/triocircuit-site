Privacy policy

This policy describes TrioCircuit version 1.0.0, its browser game and this information website. The developer is Prem Harsh.

Effective and last updated: 17 October 2026

The short version: no account is needed. Progress and preferences stay on your device. Optional private multiplayer sends your nickname, temporary identifiers and shared gameplay to the game server. The current release has no advertising or analytics SDK.

1. What stays on your device

TrioCircuit uses app or browser storage for your nickname, sound and haptic preferences, language, reduced-motion preference, tutorial state, XP, wins, best scores, daily progress and streak. If you join a room, it also saves the room code, member identifier and private session token so you can reconnect.

Solo gameplay runs locally once the game assets are available. Browser assets may be cached for offline use. Loading or downloading the game can still involve requests to its hosting provider.

2. Optional private multiplayer

When you create or join a crew room, the game server processes the room code, nickname, chosen role, temporary player and session identifiers, timestamps, game actions and shared mission state. This is used to run the room, synchronize play and authenticate your session.

People who join the same room can see your nickname, role and shared mission state. Room session tokens are kept on your device and sent to the game server for authentication; the room record stores a hash of the token. Share a room code only with people you want to play with. Do not share your session token.

You can play solo without joining a multiplayer room.

3. Security, providers and this website

The game server derives a security identifier from request IP addresses to enforce abuse-prevention rate limits. This identifier is hashed; it is not an advertising identifier and should not be treated as anonymous data. Game-server requests use HTTPS.

App platforms and hosting providers may process IP addresses, request details and technical logs as part of delivering, securing and operating their services. Their processing is governed by their own policies.

This information website is hosted on GitHub Pages. It has no contact form, advertising scripts, analytics scripts or custom cookies. GitHub may collect technical information, including visitors’ IP addresses, to operate and secure Pages. See the GitHub Privacy Statement. The browser game and multiplayer server use separate hosting.

4. Retention and your control

Private rooms become inaccessible 24 hours after creation. Expired room and rate-limit records are removed through bounded cleanup when new rooms are created. Expiry does not guarantee that every record is deleted exactly at the 24-hour mark.

Leaving a room clears the saved room session on your device. When the server receives the leave request, it removes your player membership. A leave request may not reach the server if you are offline. Related game state or security records can remain until cleanup.

Clear TrioCircuit’s app storage, or the browser game’s site data, to remove locally stored preferences, progress and session information. This resets your local progress. Clearing storage does not itself send a server-deletion request.

There is no user account or account-deletion portal in this release. Contact the developer if you have questions about data associated with a room. Never include a session token in an email.

5. AI, advertising and permissions

AI crew members use an automatic planning system. Missions use authored templates and seeded puzzles. This release does not send your text to a generative AI model.

The current release has no advertising SDK, analytics tracker, payment system or in-app purchases. It does not request access to your contacts, camera, microphone or physical location. Supported devices may use vibration for optional haptic feedback.

6. Age guidance

TrioCircuit is intended for players aged 13 and older. It is not designed specifically for children under 13. If you believe a child has supplied personal information through a room or support email, contact the developer.

7. Contact and policy changes

For privacy questions or support, email premharsh.official@gmail.com. If you contact us, your email address and the information you choose to send are used to respond to your request.

This policy may be updated when the product or its data practices change. The date at the top identifies the latest version published on this website.
