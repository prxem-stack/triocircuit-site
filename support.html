A little help for your crew

Let’s get you playing.

Learn the three roles, invite your friends or find your way back into a mission.

Start with Driver

Choose “Play with AI crew”, pick Driver and follow the first-play guide. Use left/right controls to switch lanes. Hold BRAKE to slow down and avoid traffic.

Bring your friends

Create a private crew room and share its six-character code. Friends join with the code and choose different roles. The host starts the mission; planning AI can fill empty roles.

What does each role do?

  • Driver: dodge traffic, protect the cargo and reach the destination.
  • Detective: compare all three clues — elevation, beacon signal and road condition — to select the safe route.
  • Builder: choose a bridge with enough capacity within the materials budget, then connect the relay nodes in the required order.

In solo mode, you can switch roles to try each challenge. The other roles are handled by planning AI teammates.

Why has my driver stopped?

At around 35% of the journey, the Detective must unlock the route. At around 65%, the Builder must finish the bridge. At the destination, the relay must be powered before the cargo can be delivered. These waits are part of the shared mission.

Tip: check the crew’s mission status. A teammate’s next move may be the one that opens your road.

Can I play alone or offline?

Yes. Choose any role and play with two planning AI teammates. Solo runs locally once the app’s game assets are available. The browser version may need an initial successful visit to cache its assets; a first download needs internet access.

Private multiplayer always needs an internet connection. No account is required. English and Hinglish instructions are available in Settings.

How do I reconnect or fix a room?

  1. Check the internet connection and keep the app open.
  2. If needed, reopen the game on the same device and choose the saved room-resume option.
  3. Check that the six-character room code is correct.
  4. If the room has expired, create a new crew room.

Rooms expire 24 hours after creation. Each player needs a different available role. Solo play remains available when multiplayer is unavailable.

Where is my progress?

XP, wins, best scores and daily progress are saved on the device or browser you use. There is no account-based cloud progress sync in this release. Clearing app storage or browser site data resets local progress.

Use Settings to change your nickname, instructions, sound, haptic feedback or reduced-motion preference. Your nickname is visible to people in your private room.
